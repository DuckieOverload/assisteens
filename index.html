What Assisteens Is

Assisteens is the teen volunteer group of Assistance League of Greater San Diego, focused on community service, leadership and personal growth. Members help provide school clothing, books and essentials to children and families in San Diego, and volunteer at the Assistance League Thrift Shop and at community events.

The teens run their own meetings and plan their own events. Along the way they build teamwork, communication, budgeting and marketing skills, and make friends doing it.

How they were edited

Built with Lua in DaVinci Resolve

Both edits were assembled by scripts run from Resolve's Lua console. The graphics, credits and music were made with ffmpeg first; each script then creates the project, places every clip at an exact frame and applies the grades, so the whole edit can be rebuilt from scratch in one step.

build_documentary.lua

  • 1920×1080 at 23.976 fps, the film cut into 40 shots from scene detection
  • A new title slide after the opening card and new end credits
  • Clean white lower thirds laid over the old burned-in names, with nothing cropped
  • Warm interview grade and a bolder B-roll grade (ASC CDL), with B-roll stabilized
  • Dialogue cleaned with Adobe Enhance Speech, over Toby Fox's “Home” ducked under speech
Download the script ↓

build_recruitment_short.lua

  • A vertical 1080×1920 timeline, about 30 seconds long
  • Footage cut into beats, cropped to 9:16 and graded, a few sped up 1.25–1.5×
  • Animated text and graphics with alpha, one layer per beat
  • A rapid-fire burst of four faces under a “BE PART OF IT.” card
  • Set to Toby Fox's “Hopes and Dreams”, credited on the end card
Download the script ↓

To run one: open Resolve's Console, switch it to Lua and run dofile("/path/to/build_documentary.lua"). Change the ROOT path at the top of each script first; the source footage and generated media aren't included here.
